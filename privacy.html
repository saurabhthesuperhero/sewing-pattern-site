Privacy Policy: Dart & Hem

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Dart & Hem: Sewing Patterns ("the app", package com.saikaizen.sewingpattern) is published by Saikaizen Innovations ("we", "us"). There is no account and no server of ours. We never receive your measurements, patterns or photos.

What stays on your phone

  • Body measurements and profiles (yours and family members you add), saved patterns, sew-along progress, fabric stash, notions, badges and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • Photos you add (a fabric in your stash, or "I made this") are picked with Android's photo picker or taken with the camera when you choose to, and are copied into the app's private storage. They are never uploaded. They leave the phone only when you share them yourself through the Android share sheet.
  • PDF patterns are created on your phone and saved where you choose or shared when you choose.
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your phone; turn them off any time in Settings.

What is sent over the internet, and to whom

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Google's User Messaging Platform and ad partners (free version only). To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent first, Google receives your phone's advertising ID, IP address (which shows your approximate location), app and device information and how you interact with ads. AdMob may let these mediation partners bid to show an ad, and they receive the same kind of data under their own policies: Meta Audience Network (Meta privacy policy) and Liftoff Monetize (Vungle) (Liftoff privacy policy). Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps. In regions that require it, you can change your ad consent any time under Settings > Privacy options.

Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Remote Config (Google). We use anonymous usage events (for example "pattern drafted", "PDF made", which screens are used) and crash reports to fix bugs and improve the app, plus remote settings such as how often ads may appear. These include an app instance ID, device model, OS version, country and app version. They never include your measurements, photos or names. Advertising-ID collection by Analytics is off.

Google Play handles purchases (Pro, a one-time purchase), in-app updates and in-app reviews under Google's own terms. We receive only a purchase token to confirm that you own Pro; no payment details.

Children

The app is meant for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children. A profile for a child is simply a set of measurements entered by a parent and stays on the phone.

Your choices

  • Change ad consent: Settings > Privacy options (where required).
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in Android settings.
  • Delete everything: clear the app's data or uninstall it. We hold no copy.
