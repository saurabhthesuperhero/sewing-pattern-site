Dart & Hem

Sewing patterns drafted from your measurements

Dart & Hem drafts real sewing pattern pieces from your own body measurements using classic flat-pattern drafting geometry. Print them at home as tiled PDFs with a test square on every print, check your fabric yardage, and follow illustrated step-by-step sew-alongs.

Support

Questions, a pattern that printed at the wrong size, or a fitting problem? Email superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com. Please include the project, your paper size and a photo of the printed test square if it is about printing.

Printing tips

Always print at 100% ("Actual size"), never "Fit to page". Measure the 2 in and 5 cm squares on the first page before printing the rest. Trim the dashed edges, line up the diamonds and tape.
